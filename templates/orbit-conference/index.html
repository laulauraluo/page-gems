05 / GOOD QUESTIONS

A few things
to know.

Is Orbit a real conference?

No. Orbit is original demo content for the Page Gems template collection. Dates, program, and pass prices are illustrative. No bookings or payments are processed.

Can I use this for my own event?

Yes. Replace the name, copy, program, and dates, then connect the pass buttons to your own registration service. Start with the included README.

Does the online pass include every session?

This demo offers selected streamed sessions and recordings. Edit the pass descriptions to match your real event before publishing.

What about accessibility?

The template includes keyboard navigation, visible focus, semantic sections, and reduced-motion support. Your final content and registration service still need their own accessibility review.
